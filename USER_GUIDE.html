Installing

Duo-Deck is an Android Studio project. You don't need to write any code to install it.

  1. Download or unzip the project folder.
  2. Open Android Studio, choose File > Open, and pick the project folder (the one that has settings.gradle.kts in it).
  3. Let Android Studio finish syncing. If it asks to download Android SDK 35 or other parts, say yes.
  4. On the Surface Duo, turn on Developer options and USB debugging, then plug it into the computer.
  5. Pick the Duo in the device list at the top of Android Studio and press the green Run button.

Duo-Deck needs Android 10 or newer. It works on any Android phone, but it was built for the two screens of the Surface Duo.

First launch

After the splash screen, Duo-Deck asks for permission to read your music. Allow it, and the app scans your phone for songs and loads them into the playlist.

If you said no by mistake, the app offers to open its settings page so you can turn on Music and audio (or Files and media on older Android). When you come back, Duo-Deck checks again on its own.

You can also add songs by hand: press the ⏏ eject button on the player, or ADD in the playlist, and pick audio files.

Using both screens

Drag the app across both screens to span it. Duo-Deck puts sections on each screen based on how the Duo is folded.

PostureWhat happens
Flat / bookTwo screens side by side.
Laptop (one screen raised)Switches to top and bottom right away. The hinge and tilt sensors detect it, so you don't need to do anything. Holding it partly folded like a book keeps it side by side.
One screen onlyEverything is stacked on one screen.

Press SWAP (next to SHUFFLE) to flip what's on the two screens.

If laptop mode ever shows up upside down, open the menu and choose Flip laptop view (upside down?). Duo-Deck remembers the choice.

The player panel

Display

  • Clock: tap it to switch between time played and time remaining.
  • VU meters: left and right level bars under the clock. See Visualizers.
  • Song title: scrolls when it's too long to fit. For internet radio it shows the station and, when the station sends it, the song that’s on.
  • KBPS / KHZ and MONO / STEREO: details about the current song.

Sliders

  • Seek bar (under the display): drag to jump anywhere in the song.
  • Volume (the long slider) and Balance (the short one; the middle is centered).

Equalizer

The equalizer is a 10-band EQ with a preamp. The bands are 60, 170, 310, 600, 1K, 3K, 6K, 12K, 14K and 16K Hz.

  • ON turns the EQ on or off.
  • PRE is the preamp. Lower it if boosted bands sound distorted.
  • Drag a band up to boost it or down to cut it (+12 to −12 dB). The line along the top shows the overall curve.
  • Each slider knob has a lit line across it in the skin’s main color (for example the blue of the display on a blue skin, or the green text on a dark skin), so you can spot every band at a glance. The lines dim when the EQ is off.
  • PRESETS offers Flat, Rock, Pop, Dance, Jazz, Classical, Bass Boost, Treble Boost, Vocal, Loudness, Shop Floor and Headphones.
Shop Floor is tuned to cut through background noise. Headphones is a gentle curve for earbuds.

Visualizers & VU meters

VU meters

The two bars under the clock show the loudness of the left (L) and right (R) channels, like an old tape deck. They fill from left to right using the skin's colors and get warmer toward the red end, moving with the music so the two sides bounce a little differently. Turn them off in Performance & screen if you prefer a cleaner display.

Visualizer panels

The Visualization section shows the music's frequencies live. If a whole screen is empty, for example because you hid the playlist, a large visualizer fills it. You can turn that off in Setup.

Tap a visualizer to change its style. Each one remembers its own style:

StyleLook
LED barsSegmented bars like a hi-fi display
Bars + peaksLED bars with a white cap that sits on top of each bar, then drops back down one block at a time
Solid barsSmooth filled bars
MirrorBars that grow up and down from the middle
OscilloscopeThe sound wave as a moving line
Peak dotsFloating dots only

The visualizers and meters stop when the music stops, so they don't use battery while idle.

Tap a song to play it. Tap and hold it for more options: Play now, Play next, Add to queue, Add to playlist..., Add to favorites, and Remove from the queue or playlist.

When you open an artist, album or playlist, PLAY ALL plays every song in it, + QUEUE adds them all to the queue, and < BACK returns to the list.

Favorites & smart lists

Mark songs you love by pressing FAV on the player while they play, or by long-pressing any song, artist, album or folder and choosing Add to favorites.

The PLAYLISTS tab starts with four lists that keep themselves up to date:

ListWhat’s in it
* FAVORITESEvery song you’ve marked, A to Z.
* MOST PLAYEDYour top 50 songs by number of plays.
* RECENTLY PLAYEDThe last 50 songs you played, newest first.
* RECENTLY ADDEDThe 50 newest songs on your phone.

Open one to play it all, add it to the queue, or long-press a song in it. Plays are counted when a song starts, including when the screen is off.

Internet radio

The RADIO tab in the playlist plays thousands of free stations. They come from Radio Browser (radio-browser.info), a free, community-run directory. You don’t need an account or a sign-up. You do need Wi‑Fi or mobile data.

ButtonShows
MY STATIONSStations you’ve saved. The tab opens here once you have some.
POPULARThe most-played stations worldwide.
SHOUTCASTStations from the SHOUTcast directory, another big list of internet radio. Pick TOP STATIONS or a genre, or type in the box and tap GO. Each row also shows how many people are listening (for example 1.2K ON). Tap < SHOUTCAST to go back. If the SHOUTcast directory is busy, you’ll get matching stations from the free list instead, and the bottom of the tab says so.
NEAR MEPopular stations in your country, based on the phone’s region setting.
GENRESPick a style, like rock, country, jazz, 80s, news or talk.
SEARCHType part of a station’s name and tap GO.
  • Tap a station to play it. The player’s NEXT and PREV buttons move through the stations in the list you played from.
  • On the right of each row you’ll see the station’s country, quality (for example 128K) and format.
  • Press and hold a station for Save to my stations or Add to queue. Saved stations have a * beside them.
  • While a station plays, SAVE at the bottom saves it.
  • SLEEP at the bottom of the RADIO tab is a quick sleep timer for falling asleep to the radio. Each tap steps through 15, 30, 45, 60 and 90 minutes, then off. The time left shows on the player display, and the station fades out and stops when it runs out.
  • Many stations send the name of the song that’s on. When they do, it shows on the player display, on the notification and on the widget. Other stations just show LIVE.
  • The equalizer, visualizers, VU meters and sleep timer all work with radio. You can’t rewind live radio, and End of this song doesn’t apply to it.

Many stations are in both lists, so you may see the same station under POPULAR and SHOUTCAST. You can save stations from either one to MY STATIONS.

Radio uses data. A 128K station uses about 60 MB an hour, so on mobile data you may want to stick to Wi‑Fi.

Sleep timer

Menu > Sleep timer stops the music for you.

  • Pick 15, 30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes, or END OF THIS SONG.
  • The music fades out over the last 10 seconds, then pauses. It works with the screen off.
  • While it runs, the display shows SLEEP and the time left.
  • TURN OFF cancels it.

Home-screen widget

Put a mini Duo-Deck on your home screen. It shows the song that’s playing and has previous, play/pause and next buttons, all drawn in your current skin’s colors.

  1. Press and hold an empty spot on your home screen and choose Widgets.
  2. Find Duo-Deck, then press and hold the widget and drag it onto your home screen.
  3. Press and hold it again to resize it if you like.

Tap the song display to open the app. When you change skins in the app, the widget changes to match. Play on the widget picks up where you left off, even after the app was closed.

Moving sections around

You can arrange the four sections (Player, Equalizer, Visualization, Playlist) any way you like, on either screen.

  1. Tap and hold a section's title bar (the striped bar with its name).
  2. Drag it. A glowing line shows where it will snap in, even on the other screen.
  3. Let go to drop it there.

The layout is the same in every skin. Menu > Reset layout puts everything back to how it was at first.

Setup: your own startup layout

Open Menu > Setup (my layout) to make Duo-Deck always open the way you like it.

  1. Arrange the screen: drag the sections where you want them and tap the visualizers to choose their styles.
  2. Under Show, turn the Equalizer, Playlist and Big visualizer on an empty screen on or off.
  3. Press SAVE AS MY SETUP.
  4. Under When Duo-Deck opens, choose:
    • MY SETUP to always open in your saved setup, even if you moved things around last time.
    • AS I LEFT IT to open the way you last had it.

LOAD IT NOW brings your saved setup back right away. FACTORY LAYOUT returns to the original layout and keeps your saved setup.

Your setup applies to every skin, so you can change skins as often as you like and the layout stays the same.

VU meters under the clockOn / off
Scrolling song titleOn / off
Panel texturesOn / off. This doesn't change your saved skins.
Shine and gloss effectsOn / off

Shortcut buttons: BEST LOOK turns everything on at full speed. BATTERY SAVER slows the visualizer to 15 and turns off the meters, scrolling title and textures.

These are always on: visualizers stop when the music stops, sound analysis pauses while Duo-Deck is off screen (the music keeps playing), and only the clock and seek bar update while a song plays.

Troubleshooting

No songs show up

Check that Duo-Deck has permission to read music (Android Settings > Apps > Duo-Deck > Permissions), then choose Menu > Rescan music library. Or add files with ADD.

Laptop view is upside down

Choose Menu > Flip laptop view (upside down?).

Player turns sideways when I don't want it to

If the player switches to the top-and-bottom laptop view while you're holding the Duo side by side, choose Menu > Laptop auto-turn to switch it OFF. It then always stays side by side. Switch it back ON whenever you like.

The screen goes dark while music plays

In Performance & screen, make sure Keep screen on is set to ALWAYS (the default) or PLUGGED IN.

Everything feels slow, the battery drains fast, or the phone gets warm

Press BATTERY SAVER in Performance & screen, or set the visualizer speed to 15 or OFF. Keeping both screens lit is what warms the phone most, so for long radio sessions set Keep screen on to PLUGGED IN or NEVER. The music keeps playing with the screen off, and the visualizer stops drawing while the screen is off.

A radio station won’t play

Stations go offline now and then. If the bottom of the RADIO tab says STATION ERROR, try another station. If no stations load at all, check your internet connection and tap the message to try again.

A section disappeared

The equalizer and playlist can be hidden. Turn them back on with EQ / PL, in Setup, or with Menu > Reset layout.

The layout keeps going back after I move things

Setup is set to MY SETUP, which restores your saved layout each time the app opens. Either save your new layout with SAVE AS MY SETUP or switch to AS I LEFT IT.
