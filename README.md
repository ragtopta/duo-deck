# Duo-Deck

A retro desktop-style music player for the **Microsoft Surface Duo**, with a built-in **Skin Studio**.

- Spans both screens: player, equalizer and visualizer on one, playlist on the other. Switches to top-and-bottom in laptop posture.
- 10-band equalizer with presets, stereo VU meters, and six visualizer styles.
- Drag any section to where you want it, then save it as your startup layout.
- 17 built-in skins, plus a Skin Studio for making your own (colours, shapes, fonts, textures, your own photo) and sharing them as a file.
- Free internet radio: thousands of stations by genre, country or name, using the Radio Browser directory, with song titles shown live.
- Folders, favorites, smart lists (Most Played, Recently Added and more), a sleep timer and a home-screen widget.

**User guide:** open [USER_GUIDE.html](USER_GUIDE.html), or view it online through this repository's GitHub Pages site.

## Install

1. Download this repository (green **Code** button > **Download ZIP**) and unzip it, or grab the APK from **Releases**.
2. Android Studio > **File > Open** > pick the folder. Let it sync.
3. Plug in your Duo with USB debugging on and press **Run**.

Requires Android 10 or newer.
