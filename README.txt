Duo-Deck - retro music player for Surface Duo with Skin Studio
==============================================================

How to run:
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Android Studio > File > Open > pick the "DuoDeck" folder.
3. Let it sync (it may ask to download Android SDK 35 - say yes).
4. Plug in your Surface Duo (USB debugging on) and press the green Run button.

Using it:
- Span the app across both screens: controls + visualizer on one, playlist on the other.
- The small button at the top-left of the main panel opens the menu: Skin Studio, Choose skin, etc.
- Skin Studio: change colours, button shapes, fonts, textures, a background photo and the visualizer.
  Save & use, share a skin as one file, or import one someone sent you.

Fonts: VT323 and Silkscreen, SIL Open Font License (see licenses folder).
